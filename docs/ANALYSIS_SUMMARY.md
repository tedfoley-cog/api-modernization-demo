## Coupling Ranking (highest → lowest risk)

| Rank | Service | Domain | LOC | Methods | Cross-domain calls | Risk score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **LoanService** | loan-origination | 478 | 11 | 7 | **30.3** |
| 2 | AccountService | account-servicing | 165 | 8 | 4 | 19.6 |
| 3 | PaymentService | payment-processing | 240 | 6 | 5 | 19.4 |
| 4 | DealerService | dealer-integration | 159 | 4 | 4 | 13.6 |
| 5 | ReportService | reporting | 125 | 3 | 0 | 4.8 |

`LoanService` is the **"god service"**: it is the largest by LOC and method count,
has the most cross-domain calls (7), and carries an instability of 0.7 with 8
outbound dependencies. It reaches into account, payment, dealer, and reporting
concerns — most notably it owns account creation and runs end-of-day processing
that mutates payment/account state. `ReportService` is a clean read-only leaf
(zero cross-domain writes), making it the lowest-risk extraction.

## Proposed Domain Events (12)

Replacing the current synchronous in-process calls with async domain events
breaks the coupling above. Producer → consumers:

| Event | Producer | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LoanApplicationSubmitted | loan-origination | credit-decisioning, dealer-integration |
| CreditDecisionMade | loan-origination | account-servicing, dealer-integration, reporting |
| LoanFunded | loan-origination | account-servicing, dealer-integration, reporting |
| PaymentReceived | payment-processing | account-servicing, reporting |
| PaymentAllocated | payment-processing | account-servicing |
| PaymentProcessed | payment-processing | account-servicing, loan-origination, reporting |
| LateFeesAssessed | payment-processing | account-servicing, reporting |
| AccountCreated | account-servicing | reporting |
| AccountBalanceUpdated | account-servicing | reporting, loan-origination |
| AccountDelinquent | account-servicing | payment-processing, reporting |
| DealPackageSubmitted | dealer-integration | loan-origination |
| DealerSettlementCalculated | dealer-integration | reporting |

### Key anti-patterns surfaced

- `AccountCreated` is currently created **inline in `LoanService.createApplication()`**
  — the wrong service owns account creation.
- `AccountBalanceUpdated` / `AccountDelinquent` state is mutated by
  `PaymentService` and `LoanService.runEndOfDayProcessing()` rather than the
  account owner.
- `LateFeesAssessed` is invoked synchronously from `LoanService.runEndOfDayProcessing()`,
  coupling end-of-day to payment internals.

These three account-ownership leaks are the primary driver of `LoanService`'s
god-service risk score and the first targets for event-driven decomposition.
